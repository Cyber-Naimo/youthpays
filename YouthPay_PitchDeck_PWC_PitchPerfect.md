## SLIDE 7 — BUSINESS MODEL
*(PwC Pitch Perfect: "Show the math. Revenue streams must be distinct. Unit economics must be visible.")*

**Three revenue streams:**

```
STREAM 1 — SUBSCRIPTION (RECURRING, PREDICTABLE)
Rs. 99/month per subscribing user
Collected outside the app (direct bank transfer) to avoid
App Store fee extraction (Apple 30% / Google 30%)
ARPU: Rs. 1,188/year per subscribed user
Conversion assumption: [PROJECTION — to be validated post-launch]

STREAM 2 — CROSS-BORDER INBOUND CLEARING FEE SHARE (TRANSACTION-BASED)
EMI partner charges 2.5% on inbound international transfers⁴ [PENDING EMI CONFIRMATION]
YouthPay receives 50% revenue share = 1.25% net margin per transaction⁴ [PENDING EMI CONFIRMATION]
At SOM (30K users × $250/month): $7.5M/month ecosystem flow
YouthPay share: $93,750/month ≈ Rs. 2.6 Crore/month [PROJECTION]
(Reference rate: PKR 280/USD)

Note: This is NOT MDR (Merchant Discount Rate). MDR is a merchant-side
fee on card transactions — Visa/Mastercard scheme rules prevent surcharging
this to cardholders. The cross-border inbound clearing fee is a remittance
processing fee charged on international transfers. Different instrument.

STREAM 3 — INTERCHANGE SHARE (SECONDARY)
Small percentage share of interchange from EMI partner on card transactions.
Interchange flows from merchant-acquiring banks to the card-issuing EMI.
YouthPay receives a share under the revenue-share agreement.
[PENDING: Exact rate to be confirmed in EMI partnership agreement]
```

**Unit economics at Year 1 SOM:**

| Metric | Figure |
|---|---|
| SOM target | 30,000 users [PROJECTION] |
| Avg. monthly transaction volume/user | $250⁶ [ESTIMATE] |
| Cross-border fee share revenue/month | $93,750 [PROJECTION] |
| Cross-border fee share revenue/year | $1,125,000 [PROJECTION] |
| Rs. equivalent at PKR 280/USD reference | Rs. 2.6 Crore/month [PROJECTION] |
| Subscription revenue (additional, at conversion TBD) | To be validated post-beta [PROJECTION] |

**Revenue model verdict:**

```
Subscription gives predictable ARPU regardless of transaction volume.
Cross-border fee share scales directly with user earnings activity.
Together: more defensible than interchange-only (Copper's failure)
and more scalable than subscription-only in a price-sensitive market.
```
