Questions

The important ones, answered.

Is this legal for someone under 18 in Pakistan?

Yes. The State Bank of Pakistan's BPRD Circular No. 01 of April 2026 mandates that licensed banks and Electronic Money Institutions offer teenager accounts for ages {{ages}} using NADRA B-Form verification. {{brand}} is built specifically to comply with this framework.

Who actually holds the money? Is it safe?

Your wallet and card are issued and held by a licensed Electronic Money Institution — a regulated financial institution under SBP supervision. {{brand}} is the product and technology layer. We don't hold funds directly; your money sits with a licensed institution.

Do parents have to be involved?

Yes — by design, not restriction. Under Pakistani regulation, a parent or guardian co-signs as the account's legal guardian. They get real-time alerts and can set limits. But the card is in your name, the wallet is controlled by you, and you make the spending decisions.

I'm a freelancer on Fiverr or Upwork. Can I receive payments directly?

Yes — {{brand}} is designed to solve exactly this. Your account is in your name, matching your platform profile, which prevents the identity mismatch that gets accounts flagged when teens route earnings through parent accounts. The dual-currency wallet keeps USD earnings in USD and converts to PKR when you need to spend locally.

Is there a monthly fee?
